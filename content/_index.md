Hello! I am a PhD student at the **Courant Institute at NYU**, advised by [Jonathan Niles-Weed](https://www.jonathannilesweed.com/). My research lies at the intersection of high-dimensional probability, combinatorics, and statistics, with a focus on matching and permutation problems, robust statistics, convex analysis, and statistical physics. More recently, I have become interested in the mathematics of AI safety and alignment. Before NYU, I completed my bachelor's degree at McGill University, where I worked with [Jessica Lin](https://sites.google.com/view/jessicalin-math/home). Outside of research, I love writing [poetry](https://substack.com/@shkroberisms).
