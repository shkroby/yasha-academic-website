Hello! I am a PhD student at **NYU Courant** advised by [Jonathan Niles-Weed](https://www.jonathannilesweed.com/). My research lies in the intersection of high-dimensional probability, combinatorics, and statistics. My main focus is on matching and permutation-based problems, robust statistics, convex analysis, and statistical physics. I got my Bachelors from McGill University under the tutelage of [Jessica Lin](https://sites.google.com/view/jessicalin-math/home). These days I am interested in the mathematics of AI safety and alignment. In my spare time, I love to write [poetry](https://substack.com/@shkroberisms).
